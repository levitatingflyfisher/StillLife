Nothing leaves your device unless you turn it on — and every switch that lets something leave is named, gated, and off to begin with.

Still Life is a home inventory for the conversation you hope never to have: room, container, item, value, receipt.

• Room → container → item, so you can actually find things again
• Values, receipts and photos
• Export an insurance report as PDF, CSV or JSON
• Optional encrypted sync across your own devices on your own network
• Encrypted backup with a 12-word recovery phrase

Every network feature is opt-in and named. There is no account and no company server.

Free and open source, MIT licensed.
Source: https://github.com/levitatingflyfisher/StillLife
